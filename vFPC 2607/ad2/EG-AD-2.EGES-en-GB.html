1  Airport Regulations

  1. All GA aircraft must park only on the clearly designated grass GA APRON. GA aircraft must not park on the main tarmac apron.

4  Warnings

  1. Visiting pilots should note that during periods of prolonged rainfall the grass runway can become very soft especially during the winter months.

  2. Runway 02/20: Graded hardcore runway is generally unsuitable for light aircraft with low ground/propeller clearance due to unstabilised surface and as a result it would be advisable to carry out any power checks on the tarmac apron before departure.
