EGET AD 2.20  LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS

TRULES_PROC;RULES_PROC_ID;275
1  Airport Regulations

  1. Use of Aerodrome is subject to Terms and Conditions of Use. Pilots intending to use Lerwick/Tingwall aerodrome must familiarise themselves and abide by the Terms and Conditions of use. Terms and Conditions can be found on Tingwall Airport website.

  2. Prior Permission Required. Contact the Duty AFISO for further information.

  3. Pilots must contact Tingwall Information to request engine start up.

2  Ground Movement

Not applicable

3  CAT II/III Operations

Not applicable

4  Warnings

  1. Tingwall Airport has an ATZ, but all airspace is uncontrolled. Tingwall Airport is PPR, with a Flight Information Service only. Strong winds and turbulence may be expected at Tingwall Airport.

  2. Pilots are advised to exercise caution with regards to wind shear emanating from the aerodrome and neighbouring buildings immediately to the west of the threshold on Runway 01, or on take-off from Runway 19. This is most evident when wind directions are approximately 220 degrees through 350 degrees.

  3. Birds, including geese, gulls and corvids are common. The Visual Control Room has restricted visibility from approximately 210 degrees through 300 degrees, as such, FISOs may be unaware of any bird activity in this zone.

  4. Five large wind turbines are sited to the southeast of Tingwall Airport, on Burradale Hill.

  5. Hill of Linkster, 2300 M northwest of ARP at 276 FT AMSL.

  6. Hill of Griesta, 1300 M southwest of Runway 01 Threshold at 368 FT AMSL.

  7. Ward of Laxfirth, 1600 M northeast of Runway 19 Threshold at 317 FT AMSL.

  8. Hill of Herrislea, 1700 M east and parallel to runway at 447 FT AMSL.

5  Helicopter Operations

Not applicable

6  Use of Runways

  1. Where the surface wind is at 90° to the runway, Runway 19 shall be reported as the runway in use (due to the enhanced Visual Control Room visibility).
