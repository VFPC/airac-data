EGEY AD 2.20  LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS

TRULES_PROC;RULES_PROC_ID;283
1  Airport Regulations

  1. Non radio aircraft must obtain PPR via telephone with Oban Airport Administration prior to flight, Tel. 01631-572910.

  2. High visibility jackets mandatory whilst airside.

  3. Aircraft that do not require the use of a licensed aerodrome and that wish to operate outside the notified hours of operation must comply with the Argyll and Bute Council Out of Hours Indemnity Scheme. PPR applies to all out of hours flights. Details of the scheme and an application form can be found at: www.obanandtheislesairports.com. Pilot information can be obtained from Oban Airport, Tel: 01631-572910.

2  Ground Movement

  1. Aircrew are advised to use minimum suitable power whilst manoeuvring on the Apron, as the front of the terminal building and personnel can be subject to prop wash.

3  CAT II/III Operations

Not applicable

4  Warnings

  1. Avoid overflying bird sanctuaries which extend along the coast to the north of aerodrome.

5  Helicopter Operations

Not applicable

6  Use of Runways

Not applicable

7  Training

  1. The use of the airport for training purposes is subject to prior arrangement with Oban Airport Administration, Tel. 01631-572910.
