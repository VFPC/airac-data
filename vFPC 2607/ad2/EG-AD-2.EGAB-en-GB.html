EGAB AD 2.20  LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS

TRULES_PROC;RULES_PROC_ID;427
1  Aerodrome Regulations

  1. Aerodrome not available to aircraft unable to communicate by radio unless the pilot has received prior permission.

2  Ground Movement

  1. Taxiways C and D only available to aircraft with a wingspan of less than 12 m.

3  CAT II/III Operations

Not applicable

4  Warnings

  1. HT cables 27 ft aal running east-west, 0.18 nm from Runway 14 threshold.
    High ground and trees either side of the runway.

  2. High ground and trees to the west of the Aerodrome.

  3. Aircraft landing on Runway 14 may experience crosswinds from the southwest.

  4. Operating aircraft should be aware of a right of way road crossing situated 110 m north of the Runway 32 threshold. The crossing is available H24 and is controlled by airport staff during operational hours only.

5  Helicopter Operations

  1. Helicopter training takes place on the grass parallel to Runway 14/32.

  2. Helicopter movements will also occur to/from Runway 14/32.

  3. Helicopter circuits are to be flown parallel to the runway in use at 800 ft.

  4. Helicopters must obtain specific permission to cross Runway 14/32.

6  Use of Runways

Not applicable

7  Training

Not applicable

EGAB AD 2.22  FLIGHT PROCEDURES

TRULES_PROC;RULES_PROC_ID;429

  1. Circuit directions: Runway 14 - right hand; Runway 32 - left hand. Circuit height 1000 ft agl. Helicopter and microlight circuit height 800 ft agl.

  2. Low Level circuits are permitted, but only for the purposes of practising bad weather flying.

  3. The Turn Out after take-off, Touch and Go, Overshoot or Turn On to final approach should not be below 300 ft.

  4. Non-standard procedures required for training purposes will only be permitted when the traffic situation allows.
