EGPU AD 2.20  LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS

TRULES_PROC;RULES_PROC_ID;219
1  Airport Regulations

  1. Runway departure restriction for aircraft requiring the use of a licensed aerodrome:

    1. Runways 05/23 and 11/29. Except where an AOC holder has a less restrictive State authorised take-off minima, departures when the reported MET visibility is 800 M or less are not permitted.

  2. Use of Tiree aerodrome is subject to standard Terms and Conditions of Use, which can be requested from the aerodrome.

2  Ground Movement

4  Warnings

  1. No ground signals except light signals.

  2. All taxiways are closed except between the threshold of Runway 11, the parking apron, south of Runway 11/29. Unserviceable sections of Runway are fenced off and marked with white crosses.

  3. Grass areas are soft and unsafe. Poor load bearing characteristics may be found on the runway/taxiway strips and the area adjacent to the apron. Only marked taxiway to be used.

  4. Large flocks of geese are prevalent in the vicinity of the aerodrome all year round, with increased activity between October and March.

  5. Windsurfing and kite surfing may take place on beaches near the aerodrome.

  6. Paramotor activity by non-radio aircraft takes place occasionally throughout the year on the Isle of Tiree.

  7. Wind Turbine, 563208N 0064528W, 4.4 NM/59°T from ARP, 262 FT AGL.
