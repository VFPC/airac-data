1   Airport Regulations

All GA aircraft must park only on the clearly designated grass GA APRON. GA aircraft must not park on the main tarmac apron.

2   Ground Movement

4   Warnings

  1. Visiting pilots should note that during periods of prolonged rainfall the grass runway can become very soft especially during the winter months.

  2. Runway 09/27: Graded hardcore runway is generally unsuitable for light aircraft with low ground/propeller clearance due to unstabilised surface and as a result it would be advisable to carry out any power checks on the tarmac apron before departure.
