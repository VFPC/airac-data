EGEN AD 2.20  LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS

TRULES_PROC;RULES_PROC_ID;255
1  Airport Regulations

  1. All GA aircraft must park only on the clearly designated grass GA APRON. GA aircraft must not park on the main tarmac apron.

  2. All pilots must be aware of a restriction in place for Runway 03/21. Any aircraft or obstacles on the main apron encroach the cleared and graded area of the runway strip of Runway 03/21. While aircraft or obstacles are present anywhere on the main apron, Runway 03/21 is unusable. This constitutes a variation to the licence for this aerodrome.

2  Ground Movement

Not applicable

3  CAT II/III Operations

Not applicable

4  Warnings

  1. Visiting pilots should note that during periods of prolonged rainfall the grass runway can become very soft especially during the winter months.

  2. Runway 10/28 and 03/21: Graded hardcore runway is generally unsuitable for light aircraft with low ground/propeller clearance due to unstabilised surface and as a result it would be advisable to carry out any power checks on the tarmac apron before departure.
