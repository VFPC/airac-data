EGTR AD 2.20  LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS

TRULES_PROC;RULES_PROC_ID;243
1  Airport Regulations

  1. Terms and conditions of use are available on request from the aerodrome operator.

  2. Aerodrome not available to aircraft unable to communicate by radio.

  3. Gliders, para dropping and banner towing are not permitted.

  4. High visibility clothing is mandatory airside.

2  Ground Movement

Not applicable

3  CAT II/III Operations

Not applicable

4  Warnings

  1. Distance between taxiway centre-lines and the nearest obstruction is generally only 10.5 M. Pilots are to taxi with extreme caution.

  2. HT cables, 95 FT AAL running east-west approximately 0.3 NM north of Runway 08/26.

  3. HT cables, running north - south approximately 0.21 NM from the threshold of Runway 08.

  4. Pilots are to exercise caution when operating on the helipad due to the proximity of other helicopters and aerodrome furniture. Helicopters may use the helipad provided they have procedures in place to allow safe operations. Public transport helicopter flights using the helipad must be compliant with company operating manuals.

5  Helicopter Operations

Not applicable

6  Use of Runways

  1. Only Runway 26 is licensed for night use.

7  Training

Not applicable

EGTR AD 2.22  FLIGHT PROCEDURES

TRULES_PROC;RULES_PROC_ID;245
1  Circuits

  1. Standard overhead join. Variable circuits. Fixed wing circuit height 1000 FT QFE.

2  Departures

  1. Runway 26 - After take-off maintain runway heading until crossing the M1 motorway, at the motorway junction turn right to re-cross the M1. Follow the M1 until 1 NM before the junction with the M25 then continue en-route.

  2. Runway 08 – After take-off maintain runway heading for 1 NM before turning left to avoid all built up areas.

3  Helicopter Operations

  1. Helicopters are to arrive and depart from the ATZ via specific entry/exit point. Pilots should check with aerodrome operator prior to flight.

  2. Circuit height 750 FT AGL.

4  Helicopter Route Entry/Exit Points

  1. Alpha: 514024N 0002216W;
    Bravo: 514031N 0001657W;
    Charlie: 513830N 0001930W.

  2. Helicopter arrivals to position to the northern grass at 500 FT QFE.

5  Visual Reference Points (VRP)

  1. Details of VRPs are available in the consolidated 'Visual Reference Points List' published on the 'Digital Dataset' page of the NATS AIS website, www.nats.aero/ais.
