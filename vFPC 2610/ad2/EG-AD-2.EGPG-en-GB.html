EGPG AD 2.20  LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS

TRULES_PROC;RULES_PROC_ID;415

  1. PPR: Radio (120.605 MHz), phone (01236-722822), email (ops@cumbernauldairport.org).

2  Ground Movement

  1. No aircraft is to enter the yellow hatched area at the fuel pump other than by hand manoeuvring.

  2. All fixed wing operators must ensure that aircraft are not left or parked on the main apron after fuelling under any circumstances.

  3. No aircraft to start or carry out engine runs with their tails directed towards any hangar or other aircraft.

Not applicable.

  2. Outbound and Inbound aircraft on a north westerly track to be aware of the Glasgow Controlled airspace (Class D, SFC-6000 FT) 4 NM northwest of the airfield.

EGPG AD 2.21  NOISE ABATEMENT PROCEDURES

TRULES_PROC;RULES_PROC_ID;416

  1. Runway 25 Departures: After take-off continue over golf course then turn right over the canal avoiding the old town of Dullatur.

  2. Circuit Traffic: Circuit traffic on crosswind should turn downwind and avoid overflying the small villages of Banton and High Banton.

  3. On right base for Runway 25 right-hand avoid overflying small row of seven houses at Braeface.

EGPG AD 2.22  FLIGHT PROCEDURES

TRULES_PROC;RULES_PROC_ID;417
1  Arrivals

  1. IFR arrivals intending to use the RNP IAP shall obtain a slot from Cumbernauld operations as described in AD 2.22 Section 4.

  2. IFR arrivals by aircraft unable to utilise the RNP IAP may, following prior coordination, request let down at Edinburgh or Glasgow airports before proceeding to Cumbernauld in VMC.

2  Circuits

  1. Circuit directions: Runway 25 - RH; Runway 07 - LH for all circuit traffic. Circuit height: 800 FT QFE.

  2. When circuit is active standard overhead join at 2000 FT QFE, descending dead-side to join the circuit: 800 FT QFE.

  3. During an Instrument Approach Procedure the circuit is not available to other aircraft.

3  VFR Departures Joining Airways IFR

  1. Subject to intended routing, expect to be controlled by either Scottish Control or Glasgow Approach, as appropriate.

  2. Cumbernauld Radio can obtain clearances and squawk codes from the relevant agency on aircraft start-up.

  3. Aircraft commanders are reminded that, on take-off from Runway 25, the Glasgow CTR (D) SFC-6000 FT is situated 4 NM ahead.

  4. Aircraft must remain outside CAS until two-way communications are established with the controlling authority.

4  Instrument Approach Procedures (IAP)

  1. The RNP IAP at Cumbernauld is established in Class G airspace and no approach control service is provided.

  2. The IAP must have a pre-booked approach time slot from Cumbernauld Operations on 01236-722822, which must be adhered to.

  3. Airborne requests for an IAP slot are not accepted, unless in an emergency.

  4. Cumbernauld Operations will provide a briefing document relating to the IAP. Pilots are to abide by the procedures in the briefing document when executing the IAP.

  5. Squawk code 5077 for radar conspicuity when using the EGPG RNP Approach, unless instructed to retain any previously allocated code by Scottish Control, Glasgow or Edinburgh ATC.

  6. The Pilot Brief can be found on Cumbernauld’s website www.cumbernauldairport.org for procedures and bookings.

  7. In the event of RCF, if unable to land within 30 minutes of the IAF slot time, the pilot shall divert.

  8. Light signals are not available at Cumbernauld.
