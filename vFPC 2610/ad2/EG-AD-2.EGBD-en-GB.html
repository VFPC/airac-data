EGBD AD 2.20  LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS

TRULES_PROC;RULES_PROC_ID;419
1  Airport Regulations

  1. Runway 10/28 is only to be used for instruction in flying when a qualified flying instructor acts as pilot-in-command of the aircraft.

  2. The aerodrome is not available for Public Transport passenger flights required to use a licensed aerodrome, or for aircraft unable to communicate by radio.

2  Ground Movement

Not applicable

3  CAT II/III Operations

Not applicable

4  Warnings

  1. With the exception of Runway 35, there are no QDM markings on the runways.

  2. The displaced landing thresholds to Runways 05, 23 and 35 are marked by black and white wing bars. The displaced landing thresholds to Runways 10 and 28 are marked by orange and white wing bars to give visual differentiation from other runways.

  3. An electricity transmission line and pylons, 100 FT AAL, cross the centre-line of Runway 23 1200 M from touchdown.

  4. Turbulence may be experienced from trees on the approach to Runway 23. These trees also shield aircraft low on final approach from those at the 23 hold. A good listening watch should be maintained and all appropriate radio calls should be made.

  5. Electricity transmission lines and pylons running north side and west of aerodrome 100 FT AAL (275 FT AMSL).

  6. Aerodrome Obstacles

    1. 10/Approach 28/Take-off

      Earth Bank 182 FT AMSL, 470 M beyond start of TORA across runway centre-line.

    2. 23/Approach 05/Take-off

      Hedge 185 FT AMSL, 528 M beyond start of TORA across runway centre-line.
      Trees 215 FT AMSL, 600 M beyond start of TORA across runway centre-line.

    3. 28/Approach 10/Take-off

      Telegraph Wire/Poles 200 FT AMSL, 475 M beyond start of TORA across runway centre-line.
      Trees 225 FT AMSL, 640 M beyond start of TORA across runway centre-line.

5  Helicopter Operations

Not applicable

6  Use of Runways

Not applicable

7  Training

  1. Intentional ‘touch and goes’ are not permitted.

  2. Training flights from any aircraft not home based are not accepted without permission from aerodrome licensee.

  3. Helicopter training is not permitted.

EGBD AD 2.22  FLIGHT PROCEDURES

TRULES_PROC;RULES_PROC_ID;421
