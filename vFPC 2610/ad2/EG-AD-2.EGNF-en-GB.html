EGNF AD 2.20  LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS

TRULES_PROC;RULES_PROC_ID;315
1   Airport Regulations

  1. Aerodrome is not available for public transport passenger flights required to use a licensed aerodrome.

  2. PPR is strictly by telephone and may be refused to certain aircraft types. Inexperienced pilots are to phone for advice before arriving at Netherthorpe and are to contact a member of staff for a short briefing before departure.

2   Ground Movement

  1. Aircraft should not be left on the refuelling stand.

3   CAT II/III Operations

Not applicable

4  Warnings

  1. Aircraft based at Netherthorpe operate outside published operating hours.

  2. At the weekend, when Runway 06/24 is in use, aircraft may be parked at the southern end of Runway 18/36.

  3. In calm wind conditions arrivals may be on Runway 24 and departures from Runway 06.

  4. Runways 06, 24 and 36 have displaced thresholds due to the proximity of public roads. Any pilot whose approach would result in being below 20 FT crossing the road must initiate an immediate missed approach.

  5. Overrun on Runways 18 and 36. Only 29 M of overrun strip are available on Runways 18 and 36 beyond the end of ASDA and LDA.

  6. Runways 18 and 36 Obstruction free Approach Surface Slopes are based on threshold and not strip end.

  7. Runways 06 and 24 Obstruction free Approach Surface Slopes are based on threshold and not strip end.

  8. Telegraph poles 27 FT AGL on the northern boundary.

  9. Farm and trees 39 FT AGL on the northeast boundary.

  10. Hangar 20 FT AGL on the eastern boundary.

  11. Farm 30 FT AGL on the southern boundary.

  12. Road traffic immediately adjacent to the eastern and southern boundaries.

  13. Trees 45 FT AGL on the northwest boundary.

  14. Overhead power line up to 35 FT AAL runs northeast/southwest 0.24 NM and north-north-west.

  15. Three wind turbines on the northwest boundary up to 734 FT AMSL (322 FT AGL).

5   Helicopter Operations

Not applicable

6   Use of Runways

Not applicable

7   Training

  1. 3 Counties Flying School (01909-600231) operates a Designated Training Organisation for ab-initio training.
