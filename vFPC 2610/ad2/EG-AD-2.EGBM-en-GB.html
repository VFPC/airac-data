EGBM AD 2.20  LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS

TRULES_PROC;RULES_PROC_ID;127
1  Airport Regulations

  1. Not available to aircraft unable to communicate by radio.

  2. Microlight flex wing (weight-shift) aircraft are not accepted as visiting traffic at EGBM without prior written agreement from Aerodrome Management.

  3. Runway 03/21 is available only with permission of the aerodrome operator. Pilot must have or be familiar with operating procedures for this Runway.

2  Ground Movement

  1. Grass taxiway and parking usually available during summer only.

3  CAT II/III Operations

Not applicable

4  Warnings

  1. Runway 03/21 is unlicensed and not available to operators requiring a licensed runway.

5  Helicopter Operations

  1. Helicopters refuelling area is marked by an ‘H’, located to the east of the fuel pumps. If unavailable helicopters are to refuel in the yellow box area adjacent to the fuel pumps.

  2. The following circuit directions apply for helicopter when the specified runways are in use.

    1. Helicopters approach north of centreline.

      1. Runway 08 - LH.

      2. Runway 26 - LH.

6  Use of Runways

Not applicable

7  Training

Not applicable

EGBM AD 2.22  FLIGHT PROCEDURES

TRULES_PROC;RULES_PROC_ID;129
1  Circuits

  1. Fixed wing to make standard overhead join, helicopters approach north of centreline.

  2. The following circuit directions apply for fixed wing aircraft when the specified runways are in use:

    1. Fixed wing standard overhead join at 2000 FT.

      1. Runway 08 - LH.

      2. Runway 26 - LH.

2  Communication

  1. Pilots should note the proximity of the Birmingham and Nottingham East Midlands CTA and are advised to call Birmingham or Nottingham East Midlands for traffic information before working Tatenhill Radio.
