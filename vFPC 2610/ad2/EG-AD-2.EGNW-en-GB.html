EGNW AD 2.20  LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS

TRULES_PROC;RULES_PROC_ID;603

2  Ground Movement

3  CAT II/III Operations

Not applicable

4  Warnings

  1. The aerodrome is divided by a public road and only the runways to the North of the road can be used.

  2. Hot Spot HS1 vehicles crossing the Runway 21 threshold.

  3. The warehouses of Rase and Frontier distribution on the aerodrome are not to be flown over at anytime.

  4. Aerobatics are conducted within the aerobatic box located in the South East part of the ATZ. When aerobatics are being conducted there will be no overhead joins and only live side joins will be permitted.

  5. Departing aircraft passing 1500 FT AAL within the ATZ are requested to contact RAF Waddington ATC on 119.505.

  1. Aircraft landing on Runway 33 are to backtrack in turn to the intersection and vacate for fuel or parking.

EGNW AD 2.22  FLIGHT PROCEDURES

TRULES_PROC;RULES_PROC_ID;605
1  Inbound Aircraft

  1. Inbound aircraft are requested to contact RAF Waddington on 119.505.

2  Local Flying Area

  1. Flights within the ATZ above 1500 FT AAL are subject to clearance from RAF Waddington ATC.

3  Circuits

  1. Normal circuit directions: All Runways - LH. Circuit height 1000 FT QFE. Microlight circuit 700 FT QFE inside GA circuit.

  2. Aerobatic circuit directions: Runway 33 & Runway 03 - LH, Runway 15 & Runway 21 - RH.
