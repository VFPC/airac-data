EGBN AD 2.20  LOCAL AERODROME REGULATIONS

TRULES_PROC;RULES_PROC_ID;131
1  Airport Regulations

  1. Fixed-wing operations are permanently withdrawn. Nottingham City Airport (EGBN) is restricted to helicopter operations only. Runways are not available for fixed-wing use and declared distances do not apply. All helicopter operations shall be conducted in accordance with local procedures.

  2. Public Transport or Instructional flights exceeding 2730 KG are not accepted at this aerodrome.

2  Ground Movement

Not applicable

3  CAT II/III Operations

Not applicable

4  Warnings

  1. Power cables outside the southern and eastern boundary of ATZ, max elevation 445 FT AMSL. Power cables running northeast/southwest inside northeast sector of ATZ, max elevation 328 FT AMSL.

  2. Model flying club situated approximately 1.9 NM northwest of AD at Nottingham Racecourse. Operating not above 400 FT AGL between 0930-2100 (0830-2000) all week.

  3. Permanently based cranes are situated in Nottingham City, with heights up to 400 FT.

5  Helicopter Operations

  1. Helicopters shall park on the south apron, adjacent to the Arcus Helicopters hangar.

6  Use of Runways

  1. Runways are not in use for fixed-wing aircraft. Runway lighting is retained for visual reference only for emergency service helicopters.

7  Training

  1. Helicopter Training permitted in grass centre triangle.

EGBN AD 2.21  NOISE ABATEMENT PROCEDURES

TRULES_PROC;RULES_PROC_ID;132

  1. For noise abatement, join overhead or dead-side only.

  2. For noise abatement, a 10 degree right turn after departure from Runway 21 is required to avoid Tollerton Hall and its surrounding properties. The rest of the 21 circuit remains the same.
